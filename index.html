Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Indian Skills a government website?

No. The Indian Skills is a privately operated educational platform and is not affiliated with, owned by, or endorsed by the Government of India or any government department.

Are your courses government-approved or government-certified?

Unless specifically stated for a particular course, our courses are not government-certified or government-approved. Any certificate issued by us represents completion of our course/program only.

Does purchasing a course guarantee a job or income?

No. We do not guarantee employment, business success, income, profits, clients, or any specific financial outcome. Results depend on individual effort, skills, market conditions, and other factors.

Can I get a refund after purchasing a course?

Refund eligibility depends on our Refund Policy. Please review the Refund & Cancellation Policy before purchasing.

Can I share my course account with another person?

No. Course access is intended for the purchaser/account holder and may not be shared, resold, copied, or redistributed.

Can my account be suspended?

Yes. We may suspend or terminate accounts involved in fraud, unauthorized access, piracy, harassment, payment abuse, redistribution of course material, or other violations of our Terms.

Does The Indian Skills ask for my password or OTP?

We will never ask you to disclose your password, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, or OTP through support channels.

Can I resell your courses?

No, unless The Indian Skills has expressly provided a written authorization or reseller/affiliate arrangement.

How do you use my personal information?

We collect and use personal information according to our Privacy Policy. We explain what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and applicable user rights.

What should I do if I find suspicious activity on my account?

Contact our support/security team immediately. We may temporarily restrict an account when necessary to protect users, the platform, or our systems.
